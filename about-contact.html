ABOUT SHADRECK GROUP

PERSONAL SERVICE.
HONEST WORK.

Shadreck Group was founded by Shadreck Bungeni in 2013, following his entry into the construction, earthmoving and plant-hire industry in 2011. The company has grown into a family business serving clients across Greater Cape Town, including the West Coast.

Our approach is personal and straightforward: honest communication, integrity and reliable service. With experience in areas such as Parklands and Plattekloof, we are willing to go the extra mile for our customers.
